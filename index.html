Before you begin

How to play

4 levels · 16 emails

Each level gets more challenging. You can inspect the sender and reveal the actual destination behind a link before you answer.

1

Inspect the sender

Tap “Inspect sender” to reveal the full email address. Look for misspellings or unusual domains.

2

Check the link

Tap “Reveal link destination” (or hover on a computer) to compare the displayed link with its real destination.

3

Spot the warning signs

Notice urgency, unexpected attachments, requests for credentials, and spelling or grammar issues.

Scoring

Correct decision: 10 points. Correctly identifying a warning clue after answering: included in feedback for learning. Maximum score: 160 points, converted to a grade out of 100.

Safety note: All emails and destinations in this game are fictional examples for awareness training. Never enter real passwords or open suspicious links.
